Common Questions

Accommodation FAQ

Davos week pricing applies from January 17–24, 2027 (setup days through departure). Pre- and post-Davos Summit nights are available at non-Davos Summit rates for clients who wish to arrive early or stay for Swiss Alpine experiences after the Forum. We strongly recommend booking the full 8-night block to secure the best properties.
Catering is typically quoted separately as requirements vary significantly by delegation. We offer full-service private chef arrangements, daily breakfast-only packages, or complete meal programmes. This allows clients to tailor their catering budget precisely. All GS properties are compatible with our private chef network.
Yes — all luxury chalets and corporate HQ venues are suitable for private events. Chalets can host intimate dinners for up to 30 covers. Corporate HQ venues can hold receptions of 60+ guests. Executive apartments are not designed for external events. GS Hospitality handles all permit requirements for hosted events.
We understand that delegation sizes often fluctuate. We maintain a portfolio of overflow properties and maintain relationships with other Davos property owners to accommodate changes. Notify us as early as possible and we will do our best to adjust. Our contracts include flexibility provisions for size changes up to 8 weeks before Davos Summit.
Vehicle access in central Davos is heavily restricted during Davos week. Some chalets have private garages, but GS Hospitality recommends using our chauffeured transfer service or the Davos summit shuttle buses for in-town movement. For delegations requiring armored vehicles, we coordinate with Swiss authorities for special access permits well in advance.
