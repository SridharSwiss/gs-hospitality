What is the World Economic Forum?

The World Economic Forum Annual Meeting is held every January in Davos-Klosters, Switzerland. Founded in 1971 by Klaus Schwab, it brings together approximately 3,000 business, political, academic, and civil society leaders from around the world.

Davos is not just a conference — it is five days of bilateral meetings, side events, private dinners, and pavilion activations that collectively shape global economic policy and create billion-dollar business relationships.

  • ~3,000 leaders from 130+ countries attend annually
  • 50+ heads of state and government typically present
  • 1,000+ private companies represented
  • 500+ sessions across the Congress Centre and Promenade
  • The Promenade hosts 100+ private corporate pavilions

Congress Centre & The Promenade

Understanding Davos geography is critical to planning your WEF week effectively.

🏛️

Davos Congress Centre

Promenade 89, 7270 Davos Platz, Switzerland. The main venue for WEF plenaries, workshops, and official sessions. White badge holders have full access. The Congress Centre is also where bilateral meetings with heads of state take place.

All GS Hospitality premium properties are within walking distance.

The Promenade

The main street of Davos becomes WEF's unofficial second campus. 100+ corporate pavilions line the Promenade, hosting private events, product launches, and networking sessions. Unlike the Congress Centre, pavilion events can invite guests without WEF badges.

WEF Badge Types Explained

Your WEF badge determines where you can go. Understanding badge hierarchy is critical to planning your Davos week.

Badge Colour Who It's For Congress Centre All Sessions Bilateral Rooms Security Zone
⬜ White Heads of State, WEF Leadership, Co-Chairs ✓ Full ✓ All ✓ Yes ✓ Yes
🔵 Blue Business Leaders, CEOs, Official Participants ✓ Yes ✓ Most ✗ Limited ✗ No
🟠 Orange Media, Journalists, Press ✓ Media areas ✗ Press only ✗ No ✗ No
🟢 Green NGOs, Civil Society, Academic Institutions ✓ Limited ✗ Selected ✗ No ✗ No

Important: WEF badges are issued only to confirmed WEF participants. If you need a badge, your organisation must apply through the WEF Annual Meeting registration process. GS Hospitality can advise on the application process and provide supporting documentation. Your Promenade pavilion can host guests regardless of badge status.

Security & Access

WEF Security Zone Guide

WEF Davos operates under one of the most comprehensive security arrangements in the world, involving the Swiss Army, Federal Police, and cantonal forces. Understanding the security perimeter is essential for all attendees.

  • The Congress Centre security perimeter requires WEF accreditation
  • Vehicle restrictions apply in central Davos during WEF week
  • Residential properties and Promenade pavilions remain accessible
  • Shuttle buses run between designated drop-off zones and the Congress Centre
  • GS Hospitality provides security briefing documents for all clients
  • For delegations with personal security teams: advance coordination required
Ask about security coordination

Security Zone Levels

🔴 Restricted Zone

Congress Centre interior, bilateral meeting rooms, VIP areas. White/Blue badge + security clearance required.

🟡 Controlled Zone

Congress Centre exterior, official WEF shuttle stops, designated media areas. Badge required for entry.

🟢 Open Zone

Promenade pavilions, hotels, restaurants, residential chalets. Open to invited guests without WEF badges.
