Swiss Travel
Advisory

Everything international delegates need to know before arriving in Switzerland for WEF Davos 2027.

Getting to Davos

By Air (Recommended)

Fly into Zurich (ZRH) — 2h by train or 35 min by helicopter. Geneva (GVA) is 3.5h by train. Samedan Airport (LSZS) serves private jets — 15 min to Davos by car.

By Helicopter

Zurich → Davos: 35 min. Geneva → Davos: 45 min. We pre-book slots and manage all landing permits. Essential during peak WEF week when roads are restricted.

By Train

Zurich HB → Landquart → Davos Platz: approx. 2h 15min. Swiss Federal Railways (SBB) operate first-class services. Book tickets at sbb.ch or let us handle transfers.

By Road (WEF Week Restrictions)

Only WEF-accredited vehicles permitted in restricted zones. Our armored fleet carries all necessary permits. Private cars require advance clearance.

Practical Information

Visa Requirements

Switzerland is in the Schengen Area. EU/EEA citizens need only a passport. Most countries require a Schengen visa (90 days max). Apply 3 months before travel. WEF invitees often qualify for expedited processing.

Currency & Payments

Swiss Franc (CHF). Major cards widely accepted. EUR accepted in many places but get change in CHF. ATMs available. Swiss banking is among the world's most secure.

Weather in January

Davos at 1,560m altitude. January temperatures: -10°C to 0°C. Heavy snow common. Pack: thermal base layers, waterproof boots, ski jacket, gloves. We provide full cold-weather gear packs for clients.

Language & Etiquette

Davos is in Graubünden — German-speaking region. English widely spoken at WEF. Swiss punctuality is sacred — be on time. Our multilingual team provides full translation support.

Connectivity

SIM cards at Zurich Airport (Swisscom, Salt). eSIM options available. WEF provides secure WiFi zones on the Promenade.

Health & Safety

Switzerland has world-class healthcare. Altitude sickness possible at 1,560m. Carry any prescription medications. Travel insurance essential.

Power & Plugs

Type J (3-pin Swiss) plugs. 230V / 50Hz. Universal adapters available at our chalets. We provide all power solutions for event setups.

Time Zone

CET (UTC+1) in January. Plan calls with home offices accordingly. WEF sessions run 08:00–23:00 daily — our concierge adapts.
